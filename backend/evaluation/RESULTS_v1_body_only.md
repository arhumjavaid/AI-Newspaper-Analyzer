# Sentiment evaluation results

89 real K-Electric news articles (73 distinct stories) from Dawn, ARY News, Aaj News, Business Recorder and ProPakistani. Gold labels: {'positive': 33, 'neutral': 25, 'negative': 31}. Generated by `evaluate.py`; method and caveats in README.md, error analysis in ERROR_ANALYSIS.md.

## Main results

| Method | Accuracy | Macro P | Macro R | Macro F1 | Cohen's κ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Majority-class baseline | 0.371 | 0.124 | 0.333 | **0.180** | 0.000 |
| VADER, stock lexicon, whole article | 0.629 | 0.436 | 0.583 | **0.496** | 0.420 |
| Naive Bayes, TF-IDF | 0.663 | 0.629 | 0.639 | **0.627** | 0.486 |
| Linear SVM, TF-IDF | 0.674 | 0.662 | 0.660 | **0.659** | 0.505 |
| NLP engine: LogReg, TF-IDF + target terms + domain VADER | 0.685 | 0.672 | 0.672 | **0.668** | 0.523 |
| Gemini (LLM benchmark, zero-shot) | 0.775 | 0.766 | 0.754 | **0.755** | 0.670 |

## Does the "needs review" flag catch the engine's mistakes?

The app flags an article when the NLP engine and the Gemini benchmark disagree (engine predictions here are out-of-fold).

- Flagged: 29 of 89 articles; caught 20 of the engine's 28 errors
- Engine error rate on flagged articles: **69%**
- Engine error rate when engine and Gemini agree: **13%**

## Shipped engine (C = 0.3, refit on all 89 articles): most indicative features

`t:` = term inside a sentence about the client; `dvader_*` = domain-VADER score.

- **negative**: `dvader_doc_neg`, `dvader_target_neg`, `day`, `party`, `t:day`, `t:seven`, `t:party`, `seven`, `percent`, `t:ask`, `ask`, `r`
